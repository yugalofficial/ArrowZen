## Full Description

Tap. Think. Escape.

ArrowZen is a relaxing arrow-escape logic puzzle. Every arrow on the board
wants to escape in the direction it's pointing — but it can only leave once
its path is clear. Figure out the right order, tap each arrow at the right
moment, and clear the whole board.

It's simple to pick up and surprisingly deep to master: some puzzles solve
themselves the moment you see the trick, others need real planning several
moves ahead.

FEATURES

• Classic Mode — a full progression of hand-tuned and procedurally
  generated puzzles across five difficulty tiers, from Beginner to Expert
• Daily Arrow — one new puzzle every day, the same puzzle for everyone
  worldwide, works completely offline, tracks your streak
• Zen Mode — no timer, no mistake penalty, just a calm puzzle whenever
  you want one
• Challenge Mode — Perfect Run, No-Hint, and Speed variants for players who
  want to test themselves
• Endless Mode — an unbroken chain of puzzles that get harder the longer
  you last
• A smart, escalating hint system that never spoils more than you ask for
• Full offline play — no account, no login, no internet required for the
  core game
• Light and dark themes, high-contrast and reduced-motion accessibility
  options
• Local achievements and stats — no leaderboard pressure, just your own
  progress

A relaxing logic puzzle game designed to challenge your mind and reward
every smart move.
